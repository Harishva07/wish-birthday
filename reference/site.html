What Is Wishprise?

Wishprise is a browser-based birthday surprise creator. Instead of sending a plain text message on someone's birthday, you can build a small private interactive experience — a 3D birthday world with a customised cake, floating balloons, a wheel of wishes, a personal written message, and an optional voice note recorded in your own voice.

Once the surprise is ready, Wishprise generates a unique private link. You send that link to the birthday person via WhatsApp, iMessage, email, or any other platform. When they click the link, the interactive 3D birthday experience opens directly in their phone or laptop browser — no app download, no account creation, no delay.

The experience is completely private. Share links are unguessable and excluded from search engine indexing. Every paid surprise, including its uploaded photos and audio, is permanently deleted from Wishprise 48 hours after successful payment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Wishprise completely free?

Yes. Wishprise is free to use with no hidden fees, no premium tiers, and no credit card required. The service is sustained through non-intrusive advertising on public pages. The birthday surprise experience itself is ad-free.

Does the recipient need to download an app or create an account?

No. The birthday surprise opens directly in any standard web browser — Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or the built-in browser in WhatsApp. No app download and no account is required from either the sender or the recipient.

Does it work when sent via WhatsApp?

Yes. When you paste the Wishprise link into WhatsApp, it generates a preview image that hints at the surprise inside without spoiling it. The recipient taps the link and the 3D experience opens in their phone browser — no redirect or login required.

How long is my surprise available?

Every surprise is available through its private link for two days after it is created. The message, photos, voice note, and song are then permanently deleted from Wishprise.

Can I add my own voice message?

Yes. During the creation process, you can record a voice note directly in the browser, or upload an existing audio file. This voice note plays as part of the 3D birthday experience, making it feel personal in a way that text alone cannot achieve.

Does it work for international and long-distance birthdays?

Yes — this is one of the most common use cases. A Wishprise link works anywhere in the world, on any device, over any internet connection. Many users send birthday surprises across different countries and time zones precisely because the digital format removes the distance entirely.
